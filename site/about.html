About

Hi, I'm Sam.

I'm a physics major at Auburn, a trained EMT, and someone who can't leave a piece of technology alone until I understand how it works.

Medicine

I'm a physics major, and medicine is where I see myself heading. Physics is how I like to think about things: start from first principles and work out why something behaves the way it does. A lot of medicine turns out to be exactly that.

I trained as an EMT the summer before college and have spent time on a 911 ambulance. I've also shadowed in trauma surgery, cardiothoracic surgery, critical care, the cath lab and the EP lab.

I haven't picked a specialty, and I'm in no hurry to. Every field I've spent time in has had something that pulled me in, whether that's the pace of a trauma bay, the long relationships in clinic, or the technology in a cath lab. For now I'm trying to see as much of medicine as I can.

Computers

I run a two-server homelab: a small cloud VPS and a box at home, joined by a self-hosted WireGuard mesh so nothing at home is exposed to the internet. It hosts my password manager, monitoring, and a Minecraft server for friends. I rebuilt it from scratch more times than I'd like to admit before it settled.

The whole thing is written up on the homelab docs page, configs included.
