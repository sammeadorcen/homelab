About

Hi, I'm Sam.

I'm a pre-med student at Auburn, a trained EMT, and someone who can't leave a piece of technology alone until I understand how it works.

Medicine

I'm studying Biomedical Sciences on the pre-med track, and I'm testing the waters on Auburn's physics pre-med track too. Calc II this spring will decide that one.

I trained as an EMT the summer before college and have spent time on a 911 ambulance. I've also shadowed in trauma surgery, cardiothoracic surgery, critical care, the cath lab and the EP lab.

The EP lab is where I want to end up. Cardiac electrophysiologists map the heart's electrical system with catheters and 3D mapping systems, then ablate the tissue causing an arrhythmia. It's the most technical room in the hospital: long, planned cases, and a lot of engineering between the doctor and the patient. That combination is what pulled me in.

Computers

It hosts my password manager, monitoring, and a Minecraft server for friends. I rebuilt it from scratch more times than I'd like to admit before it settled.
